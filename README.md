## The entry

**WADUH**

Show an Indonesian grandma an AI for the first time and she will not
write you an essay about it — she will text you one word: WADUH. It is
the exact sound of a grandma being confronted by technology she does not
understand, in all caps because that is how grandmas text. No
explanation, no punctuation, no second word — just the whole reaction,
instantly readable as something a grandma would actually send.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the single word to
`/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
